VPN TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Document: VPN Troubleshooting
Category: Network Support

1. Problem: VPN is not connecting

Possible causes:
- Internet connection is unavailable.
- Incorrect VPN username or password.
- VPN server is temporarily unavailable.
- VPN client software is outdated.
- Firewall is blocking the VPN connection.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Check whether the internet connection is working.
2. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN.
3. Verify the VPN username and password.
4. Restart the VPN application.
5. Restart the computer if the issue continues.
6. Check whether the VPN client needs an update.

Escalation:
If the VPN still does not connect after these steps, contact the IT support team.

2. Problem: VPN authentication failed

Possible causes:
- Incorrect login credentials.
- Expired password.
- Account locked due to multiple failed login attempts.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Verify the username.
2. Check whether the password has expired.
3. Reset the password using the approved company password-reset process.
4. If the account is locked, contact IT support.

Escalation:
Do not repeatedly attempt to log in if the account is locked. Contact IT support.

3. Problem: VPN connects but internal resources are unavailable

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN.
2. Check whether the correct company VPN profile is selected.
3. Try accessing the internal resource again.
4. If the issue continues, contact IT support.

Escalation:
The IT network team should investigate persistent access problems.
