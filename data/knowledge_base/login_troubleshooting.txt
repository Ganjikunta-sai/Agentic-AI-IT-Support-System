LOGIN AND ACCOUNT TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Document: Login Troubleshooting
Category: Account Support

1. Problem: Unable to log in

Possible causes:
- Incorrect username or password.
- Expired password.
- Account locked.
- Network connectivity issue.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Verify the username.
2. Check the keyboard Caps Lock setting.
3. Verify the password.
4. Check whether the account is locked.
5. Use the approved password-reset process if the password has expired.

Escalation:
If login still fails, contact IT support.

2. Problem: Account locked

Possible causes:
- Multiple incorrect login attempts.
- Security policy restrictions.

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Stop attempting to log in.
2. Contact the IT support team.
3. Request account unlock after identity verification.

Security:
Never share your password with anyone, including IT support.
